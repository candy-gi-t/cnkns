About the Sangam

A Home for the Nagarathar Family of Chidambaram

The Chidamabaram Nattukottai Nagarathar Sangam is a non-profit, charitable, non-political community organisation dedicated to fostering unity, cooperation and well-being among the Nagarathars of Chidambaram and beyond.

Our Vision

To preserve and protect the rich heritage and culture of the Nattukottai Nagarathar community while fostering their growth and enhancing the quality of life of all members.

    Culture & Heritage

    The Nagarathar Way of Life

    The Nagarathars — also known as Nattukkottai Chettiars — are a distinguished mercantile community of Tamil Nadu, famed for their Chettinad palaces, entrepreneurial spirit and deep devotion. From Kaveripoompattinam under the Chola kingdom, our forefathers travelled the world, yet carried their values of thrift, charity (dānam) and temple seva wherever they went.

    In Chidambaram, home of the cosmic dancer Nataraja, our Sangam keeps this living tradition close — through temple festivals, Nanal literary gatherings, kolam competitions and the passing of our customs to the next generation.
